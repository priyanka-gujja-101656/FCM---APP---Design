Daycare teacher app · full screen set · v4

Sunflower Room

Twelve screens in one world: a low sun in a pastel yellow-to-orange sky over ribboned green fields, washed back until it reads as light and depth rather than as scenery. Warm and open at a glance, silent when you are reading a name.

Twenty-one screens, covering the teacher-side features of the reference app. Scroll each row sideways.

The room01 – 06

Dashboard, roster and every way a child's presence changes: single check-in, swipe to mark absent or move, bulk arrival. One action is ever primary.

Records07 – 11

The things that have to survive an inspection or a phone call from a parent — attendance log, relocation with a ratio check, child detail, incident report, print.

Activities12 – 16

Twelve types on one screen. Logging sets the whole room first and corrects exceptions second, and the filter counts its result before you commit.

Messages & staff17 – 21

Family messaging, broadcasts that stay private, a notification list ordered by what is blocking, and the teacher's own time-off request.

One still landscape

The same faint sun and ridges sit behind all twelve screens. Nothing in the background ever moves or changes, so the app never asks for a second of the teacher's attention.

One action per screen

Exactly one terracotta element is ever live: the primary button, or the selection bar when the teacher is choosing. Never both.

Colour is meaning

Green dot is here. Amber is expected. Warm grey is away or gone home. Amber tint marks the one incident worth catching in a feed of thirty-four entries.

Bulk is a mode, not a screen

Check-in, relocate and meal logging are the same list with checkboxes. Learn it once, use it three ways.

Sheets for decisions

Check in, print and export are all three fields and a button in the same sheet shape, so a new pattern never has to be learned.

Warm above, green below

Pastel yellow and orange hold the top fifth, cool through pale blue, and settle into the fields. The sun is the only saturated thing in the whole app that is not a button.
