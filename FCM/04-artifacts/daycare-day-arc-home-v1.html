The metaphor

The sky and the arc are the same instrument. The dot rides the arc as the day passes; the gradient behind it warms, cools at nap, and goes gold at pickup. Time is felt, not read.

Cognitive load

Four things on screen: one sentence, one number, one button, three upcoming moments. Everything else lives one tap down in the tab bar.

Colour meaning

Green = here and growing. Amber = still to arrive. Warm grey = resting at home. Terracotta is reserved for the single action.
