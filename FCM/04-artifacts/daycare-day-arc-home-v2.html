Daycare teacher app · classroom home · v2

The sky is the clock

One screen, four moments. The teacher never reads a schedule — the room's light tells them where they are in the day, and the primary action changes underneath it. Quieter palette than v1; nothing shouts except the single button.

Sunrise → sunset

The gradient, the hill tint and the dot on the arc all move together. Nothing else on the screen changes position, so the day is felt in peripheral vision rather than read.

One action per moment

Check in at arrival, log during play, nap check at rest, check out at pickup. The teacher opens the app and the right verb is already the biggest thing on screen.

Colour is meaning

Green is here. Amber is expected. Warm grey is away or gone home. Terracotta is spent only on the single button and the active tab — never twice on one screen.

Nap protects the room

At 12:30 the palette cools to lavender-grey, contrast drops and the accent desaturates, so a bright phone never wakes a sleeping child.
